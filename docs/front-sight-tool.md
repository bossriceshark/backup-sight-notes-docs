# Front sight tool: adjusting A1/A2 post elevation

Zeroing iron sights on an AR-15 with a fixed front sight base means turning the front sight post for elevation. The detent plunger makes that awkward without the right tool.

## The hardware

The [front sight tool](https://www.amazon.com/Ozark-Armament-Front-Sight-Adjustment/dp/B071ZF4X3B/) from Ozark Armament is a milled metal adjustment tool for A1/A2 style front sight posts, with 4- and 5-prong compatibility. It depresses the detent and turns the post in one motion.

## Using it

- Seat the prongs over the post so the detent is pressed down.
- Turn clockwise to raise point of impact (post goes down), counterclockwise to lower it.
- Count clicks and write down your settings.

## After zeroing

Confirm the detent re-engages and the post is locked before you leave the range.
