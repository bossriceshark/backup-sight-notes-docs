# Flip-up backup sights: field checklist

A backup sight only helps if it deploys, holds zero, and stays out of the way until you need it. These notes cover a Picatinny front and rear pair, using the Ozark Armament set as the example.

## The hardware

The [flip up backup sights](https://www.amazon.com/OZARK-ARMAMENT-Flip-Iron-Sights/dp/B0HCLJM8J9) from Ozark Armament come as a front sight and a rear sight. Both are machined from aluminum and spring loaded, and they clamp onto a standard Picatinny rail. A hex screwdriver is included for mounting.

## Before the range

- Mount the rear sight close to the receiver and the front sight well forward for a longer sight radius.
- Tighten both clamps and confirm neither shifts when you push on it.
- Flip each sight up and down a few times. It should lock up and fold flat.

## At the range

- Zero the irons without the optic first, then check the co-witness with the optic mounted.
- Use the windage and elevation dials to move the group, and write down your final settings.
- Try both rear apertures: the small one for precision, the larger CQB one for close, fast shots.

## After the range

Fold the sights down, check the clamps again, and confirm the optic window is clear. With this check done, the pair is ready to use as a backup.
