# Ozark Armament flip-up backup iron sights for Picatinny

Flip-up backup iron sights (BUIS) sit on a Picatinny rail, fold flat when an optic is in use, and flip up as a mechanical backup. These notes cover the Ozark Armament front and rear pair, model FBUIS-B (ASIN B01AISP1KW).

## Fold flat when not needed

The sights are spring-loaded. Fold them down against the rail while you run the optic, and flip them up when you want the iron picture. Leave rail space so the rear sight can fold flat without hitting the optic.

## Mechanical backup alongside an optic

Irons do not need a battery or an emitter, so the pair stays available if the optic is unusable. Ozark Armament states that the sights co-witness with standard AR optics when they are up and sit low when they are down. What you actually see still depends on optic height, so check it on your rifle and zero the irons separately from the optic.
