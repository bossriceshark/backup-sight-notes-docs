# Carry handle scope mount on a Picatinny adapter

Some AR-pattern rifles keep the factory carry handle. That handle sits high and leaves no flat rail on top for a modern optic. A detachable Picatinny carry handle scope mount clamps to the handle and presents a short rail where a red dot or low-profile optic can sit.

## What shoppers call it

Listings use names like carry handle mount, carry handle scope mount, or Picatinny universal detachable mount. The job is the same: keep the carry handle and still run an optic above it.

## Before you buy

- Confirm you still have a solid A2-style carry handle, not a flat-top upper.
- Match the mount's rail length to your optic footprint.
- Check listing photos for clearance over the rear aperture.
